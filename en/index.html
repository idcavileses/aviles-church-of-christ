WELCOME · BIENVENIDOS

Church is a family.
There is a place for you.

Faith begins with an invitation: to draw closer to Jesus and learn to live as He did. We are Avilés Church of Christ in Asturias, Spain. Join us for worship and Bible study.

We are Christians in Avilés seeking to follow “the Way” of Jesus Christ and the teaching and practice of the first-century church, as recorded in the New Testament. The Bible is our guide and Jesus is at the heart of our faith. Whether you grew up here, recently arrived or are just passing through, you are welcome.

“I admit that I worship the God of our ancestors as a follower of the Way, which they call a sect.”

Acts 24:14 · NIV (excerpt) ↗

Before Governor Felix, Paul connects worshiping God with following the Way. See also Acts 9:2 ↗.

WORSHIP IN THE NEW TESTAMENT

Why we worship this way.

Jesus teaches us to worship the Father in spirit and truth. His teaching and the example of the first-century church guide our worship together in Avilés.

John 4:23–24 ↗

01

Teaching from Scripture

The first Christians devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and fellowship. We read and study the Bible together so that its message shapes our faith and daily lives.

Acts 2:42 ↗

02

Praying together

Prayer was part of the life of the first-century church. We bring our needs before God, give thanks and pray for one another, seeking a life of continual prayer.

Acts 2:42 ↗ 1 Thessalonians 5:16–18 ↗

03

Singing from the heart

We sing together a cappella, without instruments. In doing so, we seek to follow the New Testament call to teach and encourage one another with psalms, hymns and spiritual songs, singing to God from the heart.

Ephesians 5:19 ↗ Colossians 3:16 ↗

04

Remembering Jesus

Our practice of sharing the Lord’s Supper each Sunday follows the first-day gathering described in Acts 20:7. With bread and the cup, we remember Jesus’ sacrifice, proclaim His death and examine ourselves, as Paul taught.

Acts 20:7 ↗ 1 Corinthians 11:23–29 ↗

05

Giving willingly

Paul instructed Christians to set aside gifts on the first day of the week to help believers in need. His teaching guides our giving: thoughtful, generous and freely chosen, without pressure. Visitors are not expected to give.

1 Corinthians 16:1–2 ↗ 2 Corinthians 9:6–7 ↗

06

Encouraging one another

We gather to encourage love and good works. Our aim is for worship to build up the congregation, with everything done respectfully and in order.

Hebrews 10:24–25 ↗ 1 Corinthians 14:26, 40 ↗

BEFORE YOU VISIT

You might be wondering…

Is this a Roman Catholic church?

We are Christians seeking to follow the Way of Jesus Christ and the New Testament teaching and practice of the first-century church. Each congregation is autonomous. We respect our neighbors of other traditions and seek to follow the New Testament.

Can I come if I don’t know the Bible?

Yes. No previous experience or membership is needed. We want to offer a place to listen, learn and ask respectful questions.

Where and when do you meet?

We meet on Sundays from 12:00 to 1:00 p.m. (Avilés local time) in the meeting room at Centro Empresarial La Curtidora, Camino La Curtidora, 1, 33402 Avilés, Asturias. Call +34 602 59 45 41 or email idcaviles.es@gmail.com. Photographs of historic churches in the region do not show our meeting place.

I’m visiting Asturias. Is there information in English?

Yes. Select EN at the top of this page for English. Please contact us to ask about meeting languages and interpretation arrangements.
